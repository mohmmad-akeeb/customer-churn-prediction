Churn Rate

Overall Churn Split

26.5% churn rate

Nearly 1 in 4 customers churned. The class imbalance (73.5% / 26.5%) was addressed during training using stratified splits and class-weighted loss.

Churn Rate by Contract

42.7% on M-t-M

Month-to-month customers churn at 15x the rate of two-year customers. Contract type is the single strongest categorical predictor.

Churn Rate by Internet Type

Fiber Optic 41.9%

Fiber Optic subscribers churn at twice the rate of DSL customers, likely due to higher pricing sensitivity and more available alternatives.

Churn vs Retention Across Tenure Bands

Stacked bars

New customers (0-12 months) account for 33% of all churn. Churn drops sharply after 24 months, making early-tenure retention the highest-leverage intervention.

Churn Rate by Monthly Charge

48% above $110

Churn scales almost linearly with monthly charges. Customers paying >$90/mo are more than 9x likely to churn than those paying <$30/mo.

Churn Rate by Payment Method

Electronic Check 45%

Electronic check users churn 3x more than automatic payment users. Auto-pay enrollment is a strong proxy for customer commitment.

Top-10 Feature Importance (Random Forest - Gini)

Model explainability

Tenure and Monthly Charge together account for 35% of total importance. Numerical features dominate - confirming that account history is more predictive than add-on service subscriptions.

Model Selection - Accuracy & ROC-AUC

Random Forest chosen

Random Forest achieved the best balance of accuracy (85.1%), F1 on the churn class (64.8%), and ROC-AUC (0.893). Gradient Boosting and XGBoost were marginal runners-up but offered worse interpretability for the same cost.

Churn Rate by Current Offer

Offer B best

Offer B (long-term incentive) reduces churn to just 2.3%. Customers with no active offer churn at 53% - the highest single segment.

Churn by Customer Segment

Demographics

Senior citizens churn at 41.7% - 1.8x the general population rate. Under-30 customers show below-average churn at 19.1%.
